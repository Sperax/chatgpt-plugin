Get derivatives positioning

get_derivatives_positioning
  • Read-only
  • Non-destructive
  • Live external data

Use this when the user asks about leverage, funding, open interest, crowding or squeeze risk for a crypto perpetual. Returns OKX and Hyperliquid funding (8h and annualized), open interest and its 24h change, global and top-trader long/short ratios, and a crowding, open-interest-regime and squeeze-risk read. Only covers tickers listed as USDT perpetuals on OKX or as perpetuals on Hyperliquid; it does not cover spot-only tokens, options or other venues.

ParameterTypeDescriptionConstraints
symbol requiredstringTicker symbol such as BTC, ETH or SOL (letters and digits only).min length 1, max length 15, pattern ^[A-Za-z0-9]+$

Returns structured content with: hyperliquid, okx, positioning, symbol.

Build trade plan

build_trade_plan
  • Read-only
  • Non-destructive
  • Idempotent
  • Computed locally

Use this when the user has a trade idea with an entry and a stop and needs a position size, or asks how much to buy, what leverage is safe, or whether a setup is worth taking. Returns the size that loses exactly riskPct of the account at the stop with fees included, notional and margin, the R-multiple and net P&L of each target, the breakeven win rate, expectancy and half-Kelly when winProbability is given, and whether leverage would liquidate the position before the stop. It is arithmetic on the numbers supplied: it does not fetch prices, judge the setup or place orders.

ParameterTypeDescriptionConstraints
accountSize requirednumberAccount equity in USD.> 0
entry requirednumber—> 0
feePctnumberTaker fee per side in percent (0.05 = 0.05%).default 0.05, ≥ 0, ≤ 1
leveragenumber—default 1, ≥ 1, ≤ 200
maintenanceMarginPctnumberVenue maintenance margin in percent.default 0.5, ≥ 0, ≤ 10
riskPctnumberPercent of the account lost if the stop is hit.default 1, > 0, ≤ 100
side required"long" | "short"——
stop requirednumber—> 0
targetsarray of objectsUp to 5 take-profit levels. Give allocationPct on all (summing to 100) or none (split evenly).max 5 items
targets[].allocationPctnumber—> 0, ≤ 100
targets[].price requirednumber—> 0
winProbabilitynumberEstimated chance of reaching the targets, between 0 and 1 (exclusive).> 0, < 1

Returns structured content with: blendedR, breakevenWinRatePct, expectancy, liquidation, marginUsd, notionalUsd, riskUsd, side, stopDistancePct, targets, units, warnings.

Backtest signal strategy

backtest_signal_strategy
  • Read-only
  • Non-destructive
  • Live external data

Use this when the user proposes a rule-based strategy (RSI, moving-average, MACD or Bollinger conditions) and wants to know how it would have performed. Simulates the strategy on 1-3 coins over 30-365 days of daily prices with fees, slippage and gas, and returns total and annualized return, max drawdown, Sharpe, win rate, profit factor, recent trades, a sampled equity curve and buy-and-hold and BTC benchmarks. Daily candles only: it cannot test intraday rules, order-book effects or leverage, and past performance does not predict future results.

ParameterTypeDescriptionConstraints
coins requiredarray of stringCoinGecko coin IDs, lowercase and hyphenated: bitcoin, ethereum, solana, avalanche-2.min 1 item, max 3 items
costsobjectExecution costs as fractions (0.003 = 0.3%). Defaults: fee 0.003, slippage 0.001, gas 2 USD per trade.—
daysinteger—default 180, ≥ 30, ≤ 365
initialCapitalnumber—default 10000, ≥ 100, ≤ 100000000
rebalanceFrequency"daily" | "weekly" | "monthly"Cadence for dca and rebalance strategies.default "weekly"
strategy requiredobjectSignal spec. Entry fires when all entryConditions hold, exit when any exitCondition holds. Example RSI dip-buy: indicators [{id:"RSI_14",type:"RSI",params:[14]}], entry [{indicator:"RSI",operator:"<",params:[14],value:30}], exit [{indicator:"RSI",operator:">",params:[14],value:70}].—

Returns structured content with: benchmarks, equityCurve, metrics, period, recentTrades, strategy.

Scan token safety

scan_token
  • Read-only
  • Non-destructive
  • Live external data

Use this when the user asks whether a token is safe, or mentions a small cap, new launch or unfamiliar contract. Works by symbol or contract on any EVM chain or Solana and checks honeypot, buy and sell tax, mint and owner powers, holder concentration, liquidity and pair age, returning a safe, caution or danger verdict with flags.

ParameterTypeDescriptionConstraints
token requiredstringToken symbol or contract address.max length 100
chainstringChain of the token, such as ethereum, base, bsc or solana. Inferred when omitted.max length 40

Returns structured content with: address, chain, flags, market, metrics, summary, symbol, verdict.

Compare swap quotes

compare_swap_quotes
  • Read-only
  • Non-destructive
  • Live external data
  • Interactive card in ChatGPT

Use this when the user wants to compare swap rates for a token pair across DEX aggregators. Fetches live quotes from LI.FI, KyberSwap and Jupiter for any token on any chain and ranks them by output, with USD value, price impact, gas and the gap to the top quote. Give exactly one of amountUsd or sellAmount. Read-only: returns quotes, not transactions or links.

ParameterTypeDescriptionConstraints
buyToken requiredstringToken to buy: symbol (SPA, ETH, BONK) or contract address.max length 100
chainstringChain of buyToken, such as arbitrum, base or solana. Inferred when omitted.max length 40
amountUsdnumberUSD value to spend (1-100000). Give this or sellAmount.≥ 1, ≤ 100000
sellAmountnumberAmount of sellToken in token units. Give this or amountUsd.> 0
sellTokenstringToken to pay with. Defaults to USDC on the sell chain.max length 100
sellChainstringChain sellToken is paid from when it differs from the buy chain.max length 40
slippagePctnumberMaximum slippage in percent (0.5 = 0.5%).default 0.5, ≥ 0.05, ≤ 5

Returns structured content with: best, buy, crossChain, quotes, sell, sources, spreadPct.

Review wallet

review_wallet
  • Read-only
  • Non-destructive
  • Live external data
  • Interactive card in ChatGPT

Use this when the user shares a wallet address or asks whether their wallet or portfolio is in good shape. Reviews an EVM or Solana wallet: holdings and concentration, risky token approvals with revoke links, safety scans of held tokens and crisis-scenario drawdowns, ranked into findings with a healthy, attention or at_risk verdict. Read-only; it never touches funds.

ParameterTypeDescriptionConstraints

includeStressbooleanInclude the crisis stress test of the holdings.default true

Returns structured content with: address, approvals, chainType, concentration, findings, holdings, safety, stress, totalValueUsd, verdict.

Stress test portfolio

stress_test_portfolio
  • Read-only
  • Non-destructive
  • Live external data
  • Interactive card in ChatGPT

Use this when the user asks how bad a portfolio could get, wants a crash or crisis test, or asks about portfolio risk. Replays the holdings through real crypto crises (2025 tariff shock, yen carry unwind, SVB and USDC depeg, FTX, LUNA, May 2021 deleveraging, COVID crash) and returns drawdown, dollar loss and top losers per scenario, plus optional VaR, CVaR and risk contribution.

ParameterTypeDescriptionConstraints
holdings requiredarray of objectsPositions by ticker and USD value, e.g. [{symbol:"BTC",valueUsd:5000}].min 1 item, max 500 items
holdings[].symbol requiredstring—min length 1, max length 32
holdings[].valueUsd requirednumber—≥ 0
includeRiskbooleanAdd VaR, CVaR, volatility and per-asset risk contribution.—
scenarioIdsarray of "tariff-shock-2025" | "yen-carry-unwind" | "svb-usdc-depeg" | "ftx-collapse" | "luna-collapse" | "may-2021-deleveraging" | "covid-crash"Limit to these scenarios. Omit for all.min 1 item, items: "tariff-shock-2025", "yen-carry-unwind", "svb-usdc-depeg", "ftx-collapse", "luna-collapse", "may-2021-deleveraging", "covid-crash"

Returns structured content with: analysed, risk, scenarios, totalValueUsd, worst.

Analyze coin

analyze_coin
  • Read-only
  • Non-destructive
  • Live external data
  • Interactive card in ChatGPT

Use this when the user asks what a coin's chart looks like, whether it is bullish or bearish, or where to enter, stop or take profit. Returns EMA 20/50/200 trend, RSI, MACD, ATR, swing support and resistance, volume and perp positioning folded into a verdict with reasons and a plan seed (entry, ATR stop, targets) to pass to build_trade_plan. Covers coins with OKX USDT or Hyperliquid markets; it is one technical input, not a signal or advice.

ParameterTypeDescriptionConstraints
symbol requiredstringTicker symbol, e.g. BTC, ETH, SOL.min length 1, max length 15, pattern ^[A-Za-z0-9]+$
interval"1d" | "4h"Candle interval.default "1d"

Returns structured content with: candles, levels, momentum, planSeed, positioning, price, reasons, score, stops, symbol, trend, verdict, volume.
