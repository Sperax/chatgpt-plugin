Read-only by design

What it will not do.

Your keys, accounts and funds never touch it. Every tool declares itself read-only to the client that calls it.

Read the source
  • No trade execution and no trade links. Swap, perp, order and action link builders are not exposed. Quote comparisons carry no DEX links or transaction data.
  • No account access. It never connects to an exchange account, never asks for keys or seed phrases and needs no sign-in.
  • No custody and no signing. It cannot hold funds, build transactions or sign anything.
  • No prediction markets or social feeds. Prediction markets, third-party social posts and internal service status are not exposed. Upstream errors and internal timestamps are removed from responses.
  • No financial advice. Results are educational analysis. Market data is public and can be delayed. Backtests use daily candles, and past performance does not predict future results.

Questions?

What is Sperax Trading Desk?

Sperax Trading Desk gives crypto traders read-only analysis tools that check an idea against real numbers before they act on it anywhere else. It is live in the ChatGPT plugin directory and works in any MCP client.

Can it place trades or move my funds?

No. Every tool is read-only. It does not connect to exchange accounts, place orders, build transactions or sign anything, and it never asks for keys or seed phrases.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No. The plugin and the MCP endpoint need no sign-in. Each IP may send 120 requests per minute.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The plugin and the endpoint are free to use.

Where does the data come from?

Public market sources, including OKX and Hyperliquid for derivatives, CoinGecko for prices, LI.FI, KyberSwap and Jupiter for swap quotes, Yahoo Finance and Finnhub for traditional markets, and on-chain data for token scans and wallet reviews. Responses are cached for 60 seconds to 15 minutes.

Can I use it outside ChatGPT?

Yes. Point any MCP client that supports remote streamable-HTTP servers at https://chat.sperax.io/webapi/mcp/trading-copilot. Setup for Claude, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, Gemini CLI and Codex is above.

Is it financial advice?

No. Results are educational analysis built from public data. Backtests use daily candles, and past performance does not predict future results.

Who builds it?

Sperax, the team behind USDs and SperaxOS. Sperax has been building since 2019, has raised $17M and is in a strategic partnership with Microsoft.
